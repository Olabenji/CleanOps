## PASTE INTO GENSPARK

```text
========================================
CLEANOPS — MARKETING VIDEO + JINGLE
MASTER PRODUCTION BRIEF FOR GENSPARK AI
========================================

ROLE
You are a senior brand-film director + jingle producer + motion designer.
Build ONE cohesive marketing package for CleanOps:
  1) A 30–35 second hero video (primary)
  2) A 15-second cutdown (social)
  3) An original 8–12 second jingle / sonic logo that can loop under both cuts
  4) End-card lockups + caption track

Do not invent a different product. Follow this brief exactly.
CleanOps is NOT janitorial cleaning, facility management, or a generic “clean city” NGO app.
CleanOps is a Lagos State waste-collection operations platform for LAWMA PSP operators
(Private Sector Participants), their drivers, and their door-to-door collection agents.

----------------------------------------
1. PRODUCT TRUTH (DO NOT DRIFT)
----------------------------------------

Product name: CleanOps
Pilot operator flavour (OK to show): Next to Godliness Ventures — Surulere ward, Lagos
Tagline options (pick ONE primary; others as alt end-cards):
  A) “Next to Godliness.” (brand echo — preferred for Lagos PSP audience)
  B) “Run the ward from one command centre.”
  C) “Routes. Collections. Cash. One system.”

One-line pitch:
CleanOps digitizes daily waste ops for Lagos PSP operators — routes, fleet, field stops,
agent payments, attendance — so owners stop running the ward from paper, WhatsApp, and guesswork.

Surfaces to show (use attached feature images as visual ground truth):
  • Operator web “command centre” — dashboard, routes, payments, attendance, admin
  • Driver mobile “CleanOps Field” — today’s stops, complete/skip, fuel & dumpsite, offline-ready
  • Collection agent mobile — customer search, cash/OPay/PalmPay/Moniepoint payments, receipts, day totals

Workloads / pain → outcome (must appear in story, not as bullet overlays):
  BEFORE: paper route sheets, missed stops, cash envelopes that don’t reconcile, absent staff surprises, truck chaos
  AFTER: planned routes, live stop progress, reconciled collections, attendance visibility, floating fleet handoffs

Target audience (primary):
  LAWMA PSP owners, operations supervisors, and field crews in Lagos wards.
Secondary:
  Residents / estate managers (Phase 2 — mention lightly only in end card as “coming: resident pay & schedule”).

Tone:
  Confident, operational, Lagos morning energy. Warm daylight. Competent — not corporate sterile, not hype-bro.
  Language: clear Nigerian English. Light Pidgin flavour OK in jingle hook ONLY if it stays professional.
  Avoid: purple SaaS clichés, neon cyber glow, emoji stickers, floating badge spam, “AI will save the planet” claims.

Brand visual system:
  Accent green #1A7F45
  Soft mint #D8EEE0 / bg #EEF5EF
  Ink #102017
  Muted #637466
  Danger (sparingly) #A33B2D
  Radius ~14–20px UI chrome
  Typography vibe: modern geometric sans (Manrope-like), bold wordmark “CleanOps”
  Wordmark-only branding (no invented animal/logo mark unless you design a simple green geometric “C” mark — keep minimal)

----------------------------------------
2. ATTACHED FEATURE IMAGES (MANDATORY)
----------------------------------------

Treat these as approved product stills / art direction references. Prefer them as on-screen product inserts
(with subtle device framing and parallax) over inventing mismatched UI.

1) cleanops-feature-workload-fleet.png
   Hero atmosphere: early-morning Lagos fleet / crew ready for ward routes.
2) cleanops-feature-problem-to-solution.png
   Problem→solution beat: paper chaos dissolving into CleanOps digital calm.
3) cleanops-feature-operator-dashboard.png
   Operator command centre: metrics (Route Progress, Revenue, Attendance, Fleet) + Surulere routes.
4) cleanops-feature-driver-field.png
   Driver phone in street context: stop list, Complete/Skip, truck ops.
5) cleanops-feature-agent-collections.png
   Agent phone: customer search, payment capture, receipt.

If you regenerate UI motion, keep the same light-green CleanOps look, metric labels, and Lagos PSP context.

----------------------------------------
3. HERO VIDEO STRUCTURE (30–35s)
----------------------------------------

Format: 16:9 master + auto vertical 9:16 crop with safe margins for captions.
FPS: 24 or 30. Export: H.264 + ProRes (or platform best). Loudness: -14 LUFS integrated.

SCENE 1 — OPEN / ATMOSPHERE (0:00–0:05)
Visual: Full-bleed morning fleet shot (use workload-fleet image → gentle Ken Burns + light dust/sun haze).
Super (big, brand-first): CLEANOPS
Sub (one line only): PSP waste ops for Lagos wards.
Audio: soft engine idle → kick into jingle motif.
NO stats, NO address blocks, NO promo chips on the hero.

SCENE 1B — PROBLEM BEAT (0:05–0:09)
Visual: Crossfade / wipe through problem-to-solution image — paper ledgers, cash envelopes, sticky notes —
then pull into CleanOps green UI calm.
VO (male or female Nigerian English, calm authority):
  “Paper routes. Missing cash. Guesswork at the dumpsite.
   That’s how too many wards still run.”

SCENE 2 — OPERATOR COMMAND CENTRE (0:09–0:16)
Visual: Operator dashboard feature image as live UI insert (desktop mock, slight camera push).
Highlight — without sticker spam — Route Progress, Daily Revenue, Attendance, Fleet.
VO:
  “CleanOps puts the ward in one command centre —
   plan routes, watch progress, see who’s present, keep the fleet moving.”
On-screen micro labels (optional, max 3, sequential, not stacked):
  Routes · Payments · Attendance

SCENE 3 — DRIVER FIELD (0:16–0:22)
Visual: Driver Field phone image in street/truck context; stop completes with a satisfying check animation.
VO:
  “Drivers run today’s stops on phone —
   complete, skip, log fuel and dumpsite — even when the network dips.”
SFX: soft UI tick on each completed stop.

SCENE 4 — AGENT COLLECTIONS (0:22–0:28)
Visual: Agent collections phone image; receipt slides up; cash / OPay / PalmPay / Moniepoint icons appear once, cleanly.
VO:
  “Agents collect door-to-door with searchable ledgers and receipts —
   so day-end cash finally matches the books.”

SCENE 5 — CLOSE / LOCKUP (0:28–0:35)
Visual: Return to fleet atmosphere + CleanOps wordmark centred. Soft mint gradient hold.
Primary end line:
  “Next to Godliness.”
Secondary (smaller):
  “Routes. Collections. Cash. One system.”
CTA (final 2s):
  “CleanOps — for LAWMA PSP operators.”
Optional tiny footer:
  “Pilot-ready · Operator web + Field apps · Resident pay coming next”

CAPTIONS: burn-in optional; also deliver SRT. Keep captions ≤ 2 lines, high contrast on green.

----------------------------------------
4. 15-SECOND CUTDOWN
----------------------------------------

Beat map:
  0–3s   Brand + fleet atmosphere + wordmark
  3–7s   Problem→solution wipe
  7–11s  Quick triptych: Dashboard / Driver / Agent (use the three UI feature images)
  11–15s Lockup + “Next to Godliness.” + CTA

VO (tight):
  “Stop running the ward on paper.
   CleanOps — routes, collections, cash — one system.
   Next to Godliness.”

----------------------------------------
5. JINGLE / SONIC LOGO BRIEF
----------------------------------------

Length: 8–12 seconds core + 2-second stinger for end cards.
Genre: Afro-inflected modern brand jingle — warm percussion, clean electric guitar or kalimba motif,
        light bass; NOT trap flex, NOT church organ, NOT generic US tech plinks alone.
Key: major / hopeful (suggest G major or C major).
BPM: ~96–104 (walking-work pace, not club).

Hook lyrics (singable, repeatable) — choose and polish ONE:

OPTION 1 (preferred):
  “CleanOps… keep the ward clean,
   routes on time, cash on screen —
   Next to Godliness.”

OPTION 2:
  “From the truck to the till,
   CleanOps runs the bill —
   one ward, one system, one clear day.”

OPTION 3 (more Pidgin flavour — only if tasteful):
  “No more paper wahala —
   CleanOps dey run am proper.
   Next to Godliness.”

Arrangement:
  Bar 1–2: motif intro (whistle/kalimba)
  Bar 3–6: full hook with light backing vocals
  Last 1s: sonic logo — three ascending notes landing on brand resolve + soft “CleanOps” spoken tag

Deliverables for audio:
  • Full jingle mix (stereo WAV 48k)
  • Instrumental bed for VO version
  • 2s sonic stinger
  • Loopable 8s bed for social stories

----------------------------------------

• No floating promo stickers, badges, or chip clouds on product UI.

• Prefer product imagery and Lagos operational context over abstract gradients.

----------------------------------------
8. CLAIMS GUARDRAILS (IMPORTANT)
----------------------------------------

SAFE to say / show:
  • PSP operator + driver + collection agent workflows
  • Routes, payments/ledger, attendance, fleet visibility
  • Offline-minded field work / network dips
  • Cash + popular Nigerian payment rails (OPay, PalmPay, Moniepoint, bank, Paystack)
  • Surulere / Lagos ward pilot framing
  • “Pilot-ready” / “built for LAWMA PSP operators”

DO NOT claim:
  • Already live city-wide across all of Lagos
  • Full resident app is shipping today (say “coming next” only)
  • Automatic LAWMA compliance filing is live
  • “AI routes your whole city”
  • Janitorial / office cleaning / facility FM positioning
```
